Our story

Where modern comfort meets open-fire craft.

Special Dish started with one goal: elevate familiar flavors with bold techniques and warm hospitality.

From a tiny kitchen to a city favorite.

We began in 1999 serving late-night comfort plates. Today, our chefs still hand-finish every dish and rotate seasonal ingredients weekly.

Open-fire focus

Our grills stay hot all day for perfectly charred proteins.

Handmade sauces

Every sauce is blended fresh daily in-house.
